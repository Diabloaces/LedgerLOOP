Ledger Loop started when our founder, running a three-person design studio, spent every month-end re-typing the same invoices into three different tools. Nothing was wrong with the numbers — the problem was that a person still had to move them.

So we built the tool that ends that. Ledger Loop reads your bank feed, your invoices, and your receipts as they arrive, learns your chart of accounts, and reconciles them in real time. No more end-of-month scramble, no more mystery transactions.

We're a small team of engineers and ex-bookkeepers building for small businesses who'd rather spend their evenings anywhere but a spreadsheet.

Services

Three tools. One closed set of books.

Each one runs on its own, but they're built to hand off to each other automatically.

🔗

Auto-Reconciliation

Every bank line is matched against its invoice or receipt the moment both exist. Unmatched items surface immediately, not at month-end.

🏷️

Categorization Engine

Learns your chart of accounts from your first month of data, then tags every new transaction the way you would — and gets more accurate over time.

📕

Month-End Autopilot

Closes the books on schedule and only flags what's genuinely unusual: a duplicate charge, a missing receipt, a category that doesn't fit the pattern.

How it works

Connect it once. Forget it's there.

Step 1

Connect your accounts

Link your bank, card, and invoicing tool. Ledger Loop starts reading transactions the same day.

Step 2

We walk through your first month together and confirm the categories match how you actually run your business.

Step 3

Let it run

From month two on, your books close on their own. You only hear from us when something needs a human decision.
